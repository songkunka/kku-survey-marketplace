### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:** High specificity to Khon Kaen University. Incorporates KKU Mail Google OAuth, 19 KKU faculties, Mor Din Daeng Brick Red accents (`#A73B24`), Thai Baht rewards, and the Prolific-for-KKU dual-role architecture. It is purpose-built for KKU's academic ecosystem.

**Deterministic Scan:** `impeccable detect` flagged 16 advisory items in `src/components/participant/KYCModal.jsx` due to hardcoded font sizes (`11px`, `13px`, `18px`) and raw hex colors (`#92400E`, `#FDE68A`, `#B45309`).

### Priority Issues

- **[P1] KYCModal Style & Typography Drift**: Inline font-sizes and raw color hex codes bypass Tailwind classes and `DESIGN.md` tokens.
- **[P1] External Google Form Handshake Loss Prevention**: When opening external survey forms in a new tab, mobile respondents risk losing or closing the completion code entry tab.
- **[P2] Missing Quick Filters for Student Respondents**: Participants need 1-click filter chips (`⚡ งานด่วน < 5 นาที`, `💰 ค่าตอบแทนสูง > ฿20`, `🏛 คณะของฉัน`).
- **[P2] Researcher Quota & Live Balance Safeguard**: Researcher dashboard needs color-coded quota progress bars (green/yellow/red) and low-budget alerts.
